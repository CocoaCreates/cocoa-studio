## What each package includes
- **Starter:**
  - Style guide.
  - 3 opens and intros.
  - 5 in-game moments.
  - 5 crowd prompts.
  - 3 info slates.
  - 3 sponsor slates and a sponsor template.
  - Setup guide.
- **Essentials:**
  - Style guide for main and ribbon boards.
  - 5 open and intro elements: team sting, home and visiting lineups, player intro template, and a "Welcome to [arena]" open.
  - 6 celebrations.
  - 6 game-flow elements.
  - 8 crowd prompts.
  - 5 info and promo slates.
  - 5 animated sponsor slates and a template.
  - Roster updates, one revision round, and delivery before the first home game.
- **Full Season:** everything in Essentials, plus:
  - Hype open (60–90 sec) with 30s, 15s and vertical cuts.
  - Up to 15 player intros.
  - 8 partner activations, each with a board element, a ribbon version, a social graphic and a PA read.
  - 4 monthly content drops (Nov–Feb).
  - 2 themed-night re-skins.
  - Ribbon and fascia versions, plus 9:16 social cuts.
- **Championship:** everything in Full Season, plus:
  - 15 activations, each with an airing log, photo and video proof, and estimated impressions.
  - Broadcast package: open, score bug, name and stat graphics, stingers, replay wipe, and billboards.
  - 2 marquee-game skins.
  - On-site producer at 4 games.
  - Season recap video.
  - 48-hour turnaround.

## Terms
- Three payments: at signing, before the first home game, and mid-season.
- 10% off a two-year agreement.
- Activations can be billed through the school's multimedia rights partner.
- Second sport: about +40–50% of the package price.
- Rush on add-ons: +25%.
